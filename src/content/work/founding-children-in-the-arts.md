Before any event or donation drive, there was the hard, unglamorous work of actually
building an organization. I founded Children in the Arts as a real, legal nonprofit so
that everything that followed could stand on a solid foundation.

## The goal

Stand up a legitimate 501(c)(3) nonprofit that could collect tax-deductible donations,
recruit a team, and operate like a real organization — not just a one-off fundraiser.

## What I did

I served as **Executive Director**, running goal-setting and building the full schedule
of events for the year, alongside co-directors Carter Randall (communications and
operations) and Luka Reidenbach (finance and outreach).

We held regular business meetings to define our mission, drafted **bylaws** to govern the
organization fairly, appointed a **board of directors**, and launched our website and
social media. From there we expanded the team, bringing in students and community members
who wanted to help — many of whom became key to running our events.

## The result

- **501(c)(3) status secured** on January 13, 2025
- **15 members and advisors** organized around a clear mission
- A working board, bylaws, website, and social presence — the infrastructure behind every
  project that followed, from [Imagination Kits](/work/imagination-kits) to the
  [Arts for All 5K](/work/arts-for-all-5k)
