Imagination Kits was Children in the Arts' very first venture — a direct way to put art
supplies into the hands of kids in Lexington County who couldn't otherwise afford them.

## The goal

Get real creative tools to children who need them, and prove that a youth-led nonprofit
could move from idea to impact.

## What I did

We fundraised the cost of the kits through community outreach and social media over the
summer. Each kit was packed with a sketchbook, crayons, markers, paint supplies, and a
backpack-style bag — everything a kid needs to start making art.

The hard part wasn't building the kits; it was getting them to the right families. I solved
that by partnering with **Mission Lexington**, a local charity distribution center already
trusted by the families we wanted to reach. They handled distribution, which meant the kits
actually landed where they mattered.

## The result

- **50+ custom art kits** built and delivered
- **350 art supplies** donated to children across the Midlands
- **50+ children impacted** — our first real proof of concept as a nonprofit
