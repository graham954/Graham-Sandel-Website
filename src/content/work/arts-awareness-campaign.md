Social media was the engine behind everything else Children in the Arts did. It's how we
spread the message, filled our events, and made art education feel accessible to families
and kids alike.

## The goal

Spread awareness for the growing lack of access to art programs for children in South
Carolina — and turn that attention into runners, donations, and supporters.

## What I did

We ran the campaign across **Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook**, mixing two kinds of content:
posts that promoted our 5K, Imagination Kits, and donation drives, and posts that actually
taught kids about art — tips, tricks, and ideas that made art education more accessible.

I learned quickly that quality beats quantity: higher-effort posts reached far more people
and drove the bulk of our growth. We amplified our reach further by partnering with
organizations like Strictly Running and Mission Lexington, tapping into their larger
audiences.

## The result

- **50,000+ people reached** across platforms
- **20,000+ engaged** with our content
- **80.5% of engagement came from non-followers** — the message spread well beyond our
  existing audience, throughout the state and country
