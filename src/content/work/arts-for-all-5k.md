The Arts for All 5K was Children in the Arts' first in-person community event — a way
to turn awareness into action and give people a real, physical way to support the cause.
As Executive Director, I led the planning end to end alongside co-directors Carter Randall
and Luka Reidenbach and our board.

## The goal

Raise money to sponsor kids in summer art programs, and bring the community together around
a cause most people never think about: the shrinking access to the arts in South Carolina
schools.

## What I did

We spent October through December marketing and planning the race. I partnered with
**Lexington High School** and **Strictly Running** (Columbia, SC), who handled registration,
timing, and gave every runner a shirt and a shot at an award. We split awards by age group
and gender for a fair shot at the top, handed out branded medals and certificates, and worked
with local brands to give out pickleball gift certificates as prizes.

Because it was a live event, I built a contingency plan covering weather, injuries, and a
lost-person protocol that every volunteer reviewed before race day.

## The result

- **70+ runners and volunteers** at the inaugural race
- **$1,735 raised** to sponsor kids in summer art programs like Tri-DAC — hands-on
  programs spanning theatre, dance, music, visual art, and creative writing
- A repeatable event we're already planning to run again, bigger, next year
