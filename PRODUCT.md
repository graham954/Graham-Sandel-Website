# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Anyone Graham shares the site with: DECA peers and advisors, scholarship and program committees, college-admissions readers, recruiters, mentors, and his broader network. They arrive curious about who he is and leave with a clear, memorable sense of him. Mobile-first audience (links shared via Instagram/LinkedIn), so phone experience matters as much as desktop.

## Product Purpose
A personal-brand portfolio at grahamsandel.com for Graham Sandel — a Lexington (SC) High School junior, South Carolina DECA state officer (VP of Hospitality), class president, founder of the nonprofit Children in the Arts, and two-sport varsity athlete. The site is his front door: it tells his story, showcases leadership and impact, and makes him easy to contact. Success = a visitor remembers Graham as a driven, likable leader and reaches out or follows.

## Brand Personality
Three words: **Outgoing, Charismatic, Philanthropist.** Voice is confident and warm — energetic where it sells achievement, personable where it's about community, credible where numbers matter. Ambitious without arrogance. It should feel like meeting a magnetic, high-achieving teenager who genuinely lifts the people around him.

## Anti-references
- **Generic AI/template site:** no cookie-cutter SaaS landing scaffolding, stock card grids, gradient blobs, or auto-generated feel.
- **Stuffy corporate resume:** not a stiff, formal navy-and-gray company about-page. This is a person, not a corporation.

## Design Principles
1. **A person, not a brochure.** Every section should feel human and specific to Graham — real numbers, real roles, real voice — never filler.
2. **Earn the boldness.** Big type and energetic accent are backed by real achievement; confidence is justified, not decorative.
3. **Lead with impact.** Concrete results (members served, dollars raised, placements) carry more weight than adjectives — show, don't tell.
4. **Effortless to reach.** Contact and social are never more than a tap away; the site is a connection tool, not a dead end.
5. **Phone-first craft.** It must look intentional and premium on a phone screen first, desktop second.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, alt text on imagery. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for every animation. Keyboard-navigable nav and links.
