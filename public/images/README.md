# Photos — drop them here

Drag your photos into this `public/images/` folder and **rename each one to match
the filename below**. When they're in, tell Claude "the photos are in" and they'll be
cropped, optimized, and wired into the site.

Format: JPG or PNG (iPhone HEIC is fine too — it'll be converted).
Bigger is better — don't shrink before sending. Avoid blurry, dark, or heavily filtered shots.

---

## Most important

| Filename | Goes where | Orientation / size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hero.jpg` | Home page — replaces the blue "GS" card | **Portrait, 4:5** (≈1200×1500+) | You close-up, confident, well-lit, simple background |
| `og.jpg` | Link preview thumbnail (texts, LinkedIn, Instagram) | **Landscape, 1200×630** | A good photo of you — or leave it and Claude will design one |

## Nice to have — Leadership & Work pages (landscape, high-res)

| Filename | Goes where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `deca.jpg` | DECA section | Conference, on stage, competing, or with your chapter (blazer is great) |
| `student-gov.jpg` | Student government section | Presenting, a class event, or a fundraiser moment |
| `children-in-arts.jpg` | Children in the Arts section | An event, kids doing art, or you with volunteers |
| `sports.jpg` | Athletics section | A track or swim shot |

## Optional — Work / Portfolio entries

Add one photo per project, named simply, e.g. `work-spring-fundraiser.jpg`.
Match the name to the project's Markdown file in `src/content/work/`.

---

Filenames don't have to be perfect — if you name them something else, just tell Claude
which photo is which and they'll sort it out.
